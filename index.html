Questions
Do I have to buy?

No. The merch is fully optional and not required of any member of the Midshipmen Fleet.

Who can order?

Midshipmen of Classes 2027 to 2030, using a MAAP email. The Academy Edition carries the MAAP crest instead of a class seal, so it suits Class 2030 and anyone who wants plain MAAP merch.

Can I order for my family?

Yes. Add as many sets as you like, in any edition. The Academy Edition makes a good pasalubong for parents and siblings.

When do I get my items?

Orders close 4 Dec. Release is aimed before the 16 Dec break, per company, against your order number. We will announce the exact date.

How do I pay?

Scan the QR with GCash, Maya or any bank app, pay the exact total (a flat ₱20 shipping fee is added once per order), then upload the screenshot and type the reference number. Orders without a matching payment are not produced.

Are the photos the real items?

They are mock-ups made for this sale. Final items follow the same layout; shades can vary slightly between printers.

Can I change or cancel?

Message the Logistics committee before 4 Dec with your order number. After production starts, orders are final.

Where does the money go?

To the Class 2028 class fund, for approved class activities. A financial report is submitted to the DMA after distribution.
